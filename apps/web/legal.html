Montalban Navigator

Terms of Use

  1. Montalban Navigator helps visitors discover attractions and request guided tours in Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal.
  2. A booking is a request. It is confirmed only after the Tourism Office approves it and assigns a guide.
  3. Provide accurate information. Bookings with false details may be cancelled.
  4. Follow the instructions of your guide and local rules. Hiking and outdoor activities carry risk; you join at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety and belongings.
  5. Entrance, guide and other fees are paid as announced by the Tourism Office or the site operator.
  6. Schedules and attractions may change or close because of weather, safety or official orders.
  7. Do not misuse the system, attempt to bypass its security, or submit automated or abusive requests.

Privacy Notice

This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

  • What we collect: for visitors, your name, contact number, party size, preferred date and time, and notes. For guide applicants, also your email, address, valid ID number, experience, availability and interview schedule.
  • Why: to process bookings, assign guides, contact you about your tour, and evaluate guide applications.
  • Who sees it: Tourism Office administrators and, for bookings, the guide assigned to your tour. We do not sell your data.
  • Storage and security: data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) with access controls. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Retention: kept only as long as needed for tourism operations and record-keeping, then deleted.
  • Your rights: you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting the Montalban Tourism Office.

Guide Code of Conduct

  1. Provide accurate application details and a valid government ID. Approval requires an interview at the Tourism Office.
  2. Treat every visitor with courtesy and respect, including children and persons with disabilities.
  3. Put visitor safety first: check conditions, brief visitors, and stop or cancel a tour when it is unsafe.
  4. Charge only the fees set by the Tourism Office and do not solicit extra payments.
  5. Respect the environment and cultural sites; leave no trash and do not remove natural materials.
  6. Keep visitor information confidential and use it only for the assigned tour.
  7. Do not share your account. The Tourism Office may suspend or remove guides who break these rules.
